---

Starting today, I'm publishing one entry a day on Oracle Supply Chain Management: the configurations, the gotchas, and the things the documentation assumes you already know.

Two reasons.

**Writing forces clarity.** If I can't explain a setup cleanly, I don't understand it yet. A daily entry is the fastest way to find those gaps and close them.

**The road to Oracle ACE is public.** The ACE program recognizes people who share what they know. So instead of keeping notes to myself, I'm shipping them here, one entry at a time.

Most entries will be short and practical: a setting that behaved unexpectedly, a configuration walkthrough, a lesson from a real issue. Some will be longer teardowns.

If you work with Oracle Cloud SCM, stick around. Entry 002 arrives tomorrow.
